# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Lupka a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sex characteristics, sexual orientation, or technology choices.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus critique on ideas and implementation rather than people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and other people’s boundaries.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and their effects on others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, discrimination, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery and unwanted sexual attention or advances.
- Personal attacks, trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing another person’s private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing credentials, personal screenshots, OCR output, or other sensitive data without authorization.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communication. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through GitHub’s abuse reporting tools or privately through the contact methods listed on [the repository owner’s GitHub profile](https://github.com/leequixxx). Do not expose private information by filing a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, respect the reporter’s privacy, and determine an appropriate response. Actions may include removing or editing content, rejecting contributions, limiting participation, or temporarily or permanently banning a contributor.

Maintainers who fail to apply this code in good faith may themselves be removed from project responsibilities by the remaining project leadership.
