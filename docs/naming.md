# Lupka

Name for a popup app: hover over a word → hotkey → context-aware translation → the word goes to the backlog.

## Why lupka

- **“Lupka” is an informal Polish word**, the diminutive of lupa (magnifying glass). Polish app descriptions use it in phrases such as “ikona wyszukiwania (lupka)” (“search icon (magnifying glass)”). The metaphor of holding a magnifying glass over a word is immediately clear to native speakers.
- Unique as a brand, but meaningful as a word: a rare combination.
- Reads the same way in Russian, Polish, and English, and can be typed without diacritics.
- Pays tribute to the project’s roots: it began with Polish.
